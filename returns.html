Returns & replacements

Not satisfied with a product? Contact us on WhatsApp with your order number within a reasonable time of delivery and we will arrange a replacement or refund.

Products should be in their original condition where possible. Bulk and custom orders are handled case by case — we always work with you fairly.

Refunds are returned through the payment method used for the order (mobile money or cash).

Damaged or incorrect items

If a product arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, tell us right away — we replace it at no extra cost to you.
