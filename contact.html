FAQs

Common Questions

How do I order?

Add products to your cart and tap "Order via WhatsApp" — your order opens as a ready-made message. We confirm prices, availability and delivery instantly. You can also just chat us what you need.

How do I book a cleaning?

Use the Book a Cleaning page, call or WhatsApp us. We confirm the date and quote, then a trained professional arrives with supplies.

Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver in Kampala and surrounding areas, with plans to expand nationally. Delivery fees depend on your location.

What is the refill programme?

Bring your empty DeryCare container to a refill point and refill selected products at a lower price. Good for your pocket, better for the environment.

Are your products safe around children?

Yes — when used as directed. Every bottle carries usage and safety instructions. Keep all cleaning products out of reach of children.

Can my business get a contract?

Yes — product supply, cleaning contracts, bulk orders and cleaning management. Visit For Business for a tailored quote.

What is your privacy policy?

We only use your contact details to serve your orders and bookings. We never sell your data. Technology by DeryCode keeps your records secure.
