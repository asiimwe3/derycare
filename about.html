About DeryCare

A Cleaning Brand Built for Western Uganda

Products, professional services and technology — one ecosystem for cleaner homes, businesses and institutions.

Our Story

DeryCare started with a simple observation: most homes and small businesses juggle many cleaning products from many brands, with no service, no consistency and no accountability. We set out to build one brand that does it all — and does it properly. Born and based in Kyenjojo, at the heart of Western Uganda, we serve homes, businesses and institutions across the region.

Today DeryCare develops cleaning products, delivers them to your door, and cleans for you with trained professionals. Behind it all is technology built by DeryCode that keeps orders, schedules and quality accountable.
