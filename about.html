About DeryCare

Western Uganda's cleaning & hygiene company. Clean Living. Made Simple.

Our Story

DeryCare started with a simple observation: most homes and small businesses juggle many cleaning products from many brands, with no service, no consistency and no accountability. We set out to build one brand that does it all — and does it properly.

Born and based in Kyenjojo, at the heart of Western Uganda, DeryCare develops cleaning products, delivers them across Uganda, and cleans professionally for homes and businesses across the region. Behind it all is technology by DeryCode that keeps orders, schedules and quality accountable.
